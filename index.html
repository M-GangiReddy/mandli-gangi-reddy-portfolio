Hey there! I’m Mandli Gangi Reddy, a 21-year-old from Yerraguntla—a small town in Andhra Pradesh famous for its cement factories, but I’ve always been more fascinated by code than concrete. My journey started with a love for gaming, which eventually led me to tinker with computers and ask, "How does this even work?" That curiosity landed me at Saveetha School of Engineering, where I’m pursuing a B.E. in Computer Science (with an 8.7 CGPA, thanks to countless cups of chai and late-night debugging sessions). Along the way, I’ve collected skills like Java, Python, and SQL, but the real lessons came from the people around me. One of my favorite quotes sums it up: "Your support to the team and the team’s support to you makes us achieve greater things." That’s how I approached projects like the Pet Shop Management System (teamwork turned a Java idea into a functional backend) and the Voice-to-Text Converter app (where Google’s Speech API met my determination to build something useful).

But life isn’t just about screens. Last year, I traded my keyboard for a drone remote during an internship with Garuda Aerospace, where I piloted drones to educate farmers about tech—a surreal experience that taught me innovation thrives at the intersection of technology and human connection. Another quote I live by? "Having new connections with people develops our best personality." Whether it’s volunteering with the Ooruni Foundation to organize events for specially-abled children or celebrating small wins like topping my class in 2022, every interaction has shaped me. Certifications in Java, Python, and Gen AI (thanks, Oracle!) are just milestones; the real growth came from adapting, collaborating, and sometimes failing—like that one bug that took three days to fix.

Right now, I’m juggling code, coursework, and dreams of building tools that make life easier. But if there’s one thing I’ve learned, it’s that every line of code, every drone flight, and every handshake with a stranger adds up to something bigger. So here’s to more connections, more challenges, and maybe one day, a tech startup that solves real problems. Want to chat about tech, drones, or the best gaming strategies? Let’s connect—I’ll bring the ideas, and you bring the coffee!
